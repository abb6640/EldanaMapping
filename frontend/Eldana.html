About Us

Eldana was founded on the belief that aging should be accompanied by continuous dignity, connection, and full access to community life. We bridge systemic gaps in volunteer availability by creating localized, empathetic care networks that respect individual preferences and foster lasting relationships.

About Eldana

Eldana is a non-profit dedicated to bridging disparities in senior volunteerism. By modernizing preference-matching, streamlining safety protocols, and eliminating geographic deserts in elderly care, we ensure every senior receives timely, compassionate assistance tailored to their unique routine.
